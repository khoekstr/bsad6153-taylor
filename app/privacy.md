## Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

AI Research Discovery is a student project operated by Kara Taylor for BSAD 6153 at Newman University. It helps visitors explore a curated research collection and gives its owner a private reading notebook.

### Public browsing

Visitors can browse research without signing in. The app displays research metadata, source links, and saved AI summaries and interest scores. The hosting and software providers may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, and service logs, according to their own policies.

### Google sign-in

Google sign-in requests basic identity information, including your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile information. The app uses this information to authenticate your session and check whether you are the authorized notebook owner. Google handles your password. The app uses an authentication cookie to maintain your signed-in session.

The sign-in configuration uses basic identity scopes. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or Contacts. Google account information is used to provide authentication and owner access.

### Private notebook

The authorized owner can save thoughts, quotations, references, and reading progress in the project's Supabase database. Public visitors cannot access the private notebook. Notebook data is retained until the owner arranges its removal. Signing out ends access through that browser session and does not delete saved notes.

### Providers and data use

Render hosts the website, Supabase stores the project's data, and Google provides sign-in. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. Public research links take you to external sites with their own privacy practices. The current app displays saved AI assessments and does not make LLM calls when you browse or save notes. Google identity information and private notes are not displayed in the public research views or sent to an LLM by these features.

### Questions and deletion requests

Contact Kara Taylor at taylor.kara25@gmail.com with questions or requests to remove notebook data. You can also revoke this app's Google access in your Google Account settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete notebook entries.
